Containers Hamlet / Community / Resident scenarios

Illustrative, not representative

Start from a resident, not a floor plan.

These six situations are prompts for design and community decisions. They do not assume incomes, household structures or care needs beyond what is stated, and they are not a waiting list or an application.

A 20-foot seasonal cabin is not a universal home. Juniper is presented as compact seasonal accommodation. A resident with changing mobility, full-time work or significant storage needs may need a larger or redesigned home.

40-ft single

Living alone, year-round

A permanent resident who wants a compact home, a short walk to the lodge and a clear maintenance routine.

Home concept
Cedar
Space
~27 m² clear plus porch; one sleeping room, living/kitchen and bathroom.
Storage
A small exterior locker, porch storage and a loft/bed storage plan. No basement.
Winter
Cold-climate heat pump, vestibule, drain-down and a snow-clearing routine.
Accessibility
Step-free porch route, but the current bathroom does not fit a 1.5 m turning circle.
Privacy
A separate clearing and screened porch; the lodge is a short walk away.
Work and connectivity
A desk in the living room; connectivity needs a real service test, not a map promise.
Shared facilities
Lodge laundry, workshop, guest room and community meals.
Responsibilities
Path clearing, shared-system checks, a buddy check-in and a role in the fire plan.
Questions that remain unresolved

Overnight care, illness, isolation and how a solo resident gets help quickly.

Two-container pair

A couple working remotely

Two people who need reliable work space, power and bandwidth without turning the home into an office.

Home concept
Birch
Space
~50 m² clear; separate work corner or second-module studio; two screens and a call space.
Storage
Files, equipment and bikes need dedicated storage; the porch is not a warehouse.
Winter
Heat pump, balanced ventilation and a power/backup plan for workdays.
Accessibility
Step-free route; verify door widths, thresholds and a place for mobility equipment.
Privacy
Two people need acoustic separation; the pair plan has to be tested, not assumed.
Work and connectivity
A service test for fibre/fixed wireless; shared lodge meeting room as overflow.
Shared facilities
Lodge meeting room, guest room, workshop and laundry.
Responsibilities
Shared internet contract, backup power, snow clearing and quiet-hours norms.
Questions that remain unresolved

Bandwidth, latency, backup power and whether remote work is compatible with a seasonal community.

20-ft compact

A seasonal resident

A summer and shoulder-season resident who does not need a year-round home and should not pretend a 20-ft cabin is one.

Home concept
Juniper
Space
~12.8 m² clear; one compact bedroom, bathroom and living/kitchen.
Storage
Very limited; a separate locker and the lodge store are essential.
Winter
The compact cabin is not presented as a year-round accessible home; water, heat and road access need a winter strategy.
Accessibility
Compact bathroom and tight circulation; not suitable for a wheelchair user without redesign.
Privacy
A low, juniper-edged site with a separate porch view.
Work and connectivity
Occasional desk use only; do not assume a permanent office.
Shared facilities
Lodge laundry, guest space, tools and the main gathering space.
Responsibilities
Seasonal opening/closing, water drain-down, wildlife-proofing and shared fees.
Questions that remain unresolved

Whether winter occupation is permitted, insurable and safe; short-term rental rules.

40-ft single, accessible redesign required

Planning for changing mobility

An older resident who wants to stay in the community as mobility changes, without assuming the current compact plan is accessible.

Home concept
Pine
Space
A longer room or a different plan is needed for a 1.5 m turning circle; the current bathroom fails.
Storage
Reachable storage, no ladder-only storage, space for mobility equipment.
Winter
Reliable heat, backup power, a clear accessible path and a place to wait for help.
Accessibility
Step-free entry is only the start; bathroom, sleeping and kitchen clearances need redesign.
Privacy
A quiet site, but the resident needs to be reachable in an emergency.
Work and connectivity
Optional; a seated work surface and good light.
Shared facilities
Lodge universal washroom, meals and guest space; not a substitute for personal care.
Responsibilities
Accessible path maintenance, emergency check-in and a personal support plan.
Questions that remain unresolved

Personal care, funding, home modifications, evacuation and aging in place without a care facility.

Two-container pair

A household that hosts guests

A resident who wants occasional family and friends to stay without letting visitors take over the hamlet.

Home concept
Moss
Space
A second sleeping room or a convertible studio; a guest bathroom is better than a shared one.
Storage
Guest bedding, chairs, parking and outdoor gear need a home.
Winter
Extra heat and water demand; a guest wing should be closable.
Accessibility
Guest route and bathroom should be step-free; visitors may have different needs.
Privacy
Visitor rules, quiet hours and a clear boundary around the resident's rooms.
Work and connectivity
Noise and call conflicts; guests need a place to work if they stay.
Shared facilities
Lodge guest room, parking, laundry and gathering space.
Responsibilities
Booking rules, cleaning, insurance, water use and neighbourhood courtesy.
Questions that remain unresolved

Short-term rental versus private hosting; parking, septic load and insurance.

Two-container pair

Gardening and land stewardship

A resident who wants to grow food, compost and care for the forest, with realistic water and wildlife constraints.

Home concept
Fern
Space
A home plus a small mudroom/utility zone for tools and produce.
Storage
Tool storage, harvest storage, potting bench and a place to dry boots.
Winter
Seed starting, freeze protection and a water plan that does not empty the fire reserve.
Accessibility
Raised beds and a firm route; gardening still needs a seated option.
Privacy
Garden is shared or semi-private; clear rules prevent quiet encroachment.
Work and connectivity
A seasonal work pattern; not assumed to be a paid farm.
Shared facilities
Community garden, compost, tool library, lodge kitchen and water.
Responsibilities
Watering rota, compost management, invasive-species control and deer/fire clearance.
Questions that remain unresolved

Soil contamination, water rights, food safety, wildlife and who owns the harvest.
