Plan, not results

A usability test a small team can actually run.

This is a proposed study. No representative residents have completed it yet. Do not describe it as completed validation until real participants have taken part and the results are published.

No personal data collection. Use anonymous task codes. Do not record health, income or immigration status. Obtain consent for any recording and offer a non-recorded option.

Tasks

  1. Find a home suitable for year-round living.
  2. Determine what a resident would own and what the land trust would own.
  3. Identify the shared operating costs and who pays them.
  4. Find the accessibility limitations of the compact homes.
  5. Understand whether short-term rentals are permitted.
  6. Locate the water and wastewater assumptions.
  7. Customize a container design and download the result.
  8. Find a way to contribute without creating an account.

Script outline

  1. Explain the fictional status and that this is not a sales process.
  2. Ask for consent to observe; offer to stop at any time.
  3. Read each task without pointing to the answer.
  4. Ask the participant to think aloud, but do not lead.
  5. Record completion, time, wrong turns, misunderstood words and accessibility barriers.
  6. Debrief: what was believable, what was missing, what felt like sales pressure?

Participants

Recruit a small mix of people: at least one keyboard-only or screen-reader user, one older person, one person who has lived rurally, and one person who has not. Do not ask them to represent a whole group. Record only what they consent to share.
