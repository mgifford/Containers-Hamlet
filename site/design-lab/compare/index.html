Thermal strengths

  • Continuous external wall insulation (150 mm average) reduces thermal bridging.
  • Roof insulation is 250 mm in the concept.
  • Floor insulation is 200 mm in the concept.
  • Triple glazing and thermally broken frames are the stated intent.
  • External shading is part of the intent: fixed overhang and deciduous planting.
  • Heat-recovery ventilation is included as an intent, supporting winter ventilation and moisture control.
  • A cold-climate heat pump is the stated heating intent; sizing and backup are not calculated.
  • The rainscreen is ventilated and the cladding replaceable.
  • Thermal-bridge intent: continuous external insulation with thermal breaks at openings, pile caps and service penetrations.

Thermal risks

  • Glazing is about 34% of nominal conditioned area; summer overheating and winter heat loss need modelling.

Accessibility considerations

  • Step-free porch route is intended; bathroom and sleeping turning space need redesign.
  • Bathroom Bathroom is 2.1 m long. Verify a 1.5 m turning circle, transfer space, grab bars and door clearance against CSA B651 and the applicable code.
  • Door swings, circulation widths, furniture and fixture clearances have not been verified.

Structural / installation review

  • m1 is about 2.8 m wide and 3.3 m high after insulation and roof. Oversize transport permits and route surveys are likely.
  • A 40-foot module requires oversize transport planning, route surveys and permits.
  • PV and green roofs add dead load, snow drift, drainage and fire requirements. Roof structure and waterproofing need engineering review.

Thermal strengths

  • Continuous external wall insulation (200 mm average) reduces thermal bridging.
  • Roof insulation is 350 mm in the concept.
  • Floor insulation is 250 mm in the concept.
  • Triple glazing and thermally broken frames are the stated intent.
  • External shading is part of the intent: fixed overhang, covered veranda and deciduous planting.
  • Heat-recovery ventilation is included as an intent, supporting winter ventilation and moisture control.
  • A cold-climate heat pump is the stated heating intent; sizing and backup are not calculated.
  • The rainscreen is ventilated and the cladding replaceable.
  • Thermal-bridge intent: continuous external insulation with thermal breaks at openings, pile caps and service penetrations.
  • The central hall/veranda is modelled with insulated sliding/folding glazing intent, not an uninsulated garage door.

Thermal risks

  • Glazing is about 33% of nominal conditioned area; summer overheating and winter heat loss need modelling.
  • Green or biosolar roof adds saturated load, drainage and maintenance complexity; it is not a substitute for roof insulation.

Accessibility considerations

  • Step-free hall and veranda intended; two accessible washrooms, one with an adult-size change table; door widths, turning spaces and counter heights need verification.
  • Bathroom U. WC is 2 m long. Verify a 1.5 m turning circle, transfer space, grab bars and door clearance against CSA B651 and the applicable code.
  • Bathroom A. WC is 1.5 m long. Verify a 1.5 m turning circle, transfer space, grab bars and door clearance against CSA B651 and the applicable code.
  • Bathroom A. WC is 2 m long. Verify a 1.5 m turning circle, transfer space, grab bars and door clearance against CSA B651 and the applicable code.
  • Door swings, circulation widths, furniture and fixture clearances have not been verified.

  • m1 has a green or biosolar roof. Saturated weight, snow, drainage, waterproofing and fire need engineering review.
