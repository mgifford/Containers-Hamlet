Woodland Courtyard

A north module holds kitchen and living; two rotated side modules hold sleeping and work. Together they enclose a protected courtyard, with the screened porch facing into it.

Shading and seasonal notes

  • External shading intent: fixed overhang and deciduous planting.
  • Freestanding trellis on the north face: seasonal climber shading is not calculated.
  • Modular living wall on the east face (evergreen): approximate screening, not an energy or daylight calculation.
  • Evergreen privacy screen on the west face (evergreen): approximate screening, not an energy or daylight calculation.

Privacy and screening

  • Modular living wall is 2 m long and 2.6 m high; visual privacy is not scored.
  • Evergreen privacy screen is 4 m long and 3 m high; visual privacy is not scored.

Maintenance

  • Trellis t1: prune, secure and inspect the independent frame.
  • Modular living wall v1: Quarterly module and irrigation checks; replace failed modules; irrigation: drip with recirculation.
  • Evergreen privacy screen v2: Annual pruning, tie and post inspection; irrigation: establishment watering only.

Envelope assembly

hybrid · version 1.0. The diagram is explanatory, not a construction detail; it is not code-compliant or moisture-safe without engineering review.

Layers

  • steel container wall — 3 mm (structure)
  • air and vapour control layer — 1 mm (air/vapour)
  • wood-fibre board — 100 mm (insulation)
  • mineral wool — 100 mm (insulation)
  • continuous thermal break / insulation — 10 mm (thermal break)
  • ventilated drained cavity — 25 mm (rainscreen)
  • timber cladding — 20 mm (cladding)

Condensation, fire, moisture, fastening, structural penetrations and maintenance all need project review. The designer does not claim this assembly is compliant or moisture-safe.

Foundation and ground

helical-piles · 0.6 m clearance · no compaction intent · independent decks: yes.

Elevated floor on engineered helical piles. Confirm soil, bedrock, frost uplift, corrosion, lateral capacity, slope stability and permit requirements before construction. Geotechnical, frost-uplift, corrosion, lateral, slope, permit and conservation-authority review are required. No fill, slab or compaction is the intent; the construction sequence still needs an arborist and erosion-control plan.

Solar tilt and azimuth

39° tilt · 180° azimuth · year-round strategy. True south; confirm magnetic declination, shading, snow shedding and structural fixing.

Ottawa reference: the year-round fixed range is 36–39° (SolarCalculator.CA reports 39° optimal), winter is about 60° and summer about 30°, facing true south within a few degrees. No energy production, snow-shedding, wind-uplift or code compliance is calculated.

Vegetation systems

  • Freestanding trellis on the west face, 2 × 3.2 m; independent support; Trellis on the outer west end.
  • Modular living wall on the east face, 2 × 2.6 m; evergreen; clearance 0.5 m; independent galvanised frame on screw piles; irrigation: drip with recirculation; maintenance: Quarterly module and irrigation checks; replace failed modules.
  • Evergreen privacy screen on the south face, 4 × 3 m; evergreen; clearance 0.9 m; independent posts and root barrier; irrigation: establishment watering only; maintenance: Annual pruning, tie and post inspection.

Planting is independently supported where practical. Fire, moisture, structural loads, irrigation, plant selection and maintenance require professional review. Compare the four vegetation systems.
