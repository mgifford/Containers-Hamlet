Accessible Garden Home

A wide, low single-storey home made of two 20-foot container modules placed side by side to create a double-width plan. The extra width supports a step-free entrance, a 1.2 m circulation route and a bathroom with a 1.5 m turning circle. The bathroom and kitchen remain concept layouts; the shared-path grades have not been surveyed.

Heat-pump assessment

Design heat load at −30 °C: 1.9 kW. Annual heating 1991 kWh thermal; annual cooling 784 kWh. An air-source heat pump would use about 1362 kWh/year for heating and 424 kWh/year for cooling, in addition to the household load.

The existing household demand and reference array are unchanged. Full-time heat-pump heating would require about 35 additional winter-critical panels (69.9 m²), 56 kWh of additional battery for three days and a combined peak of 5.3 kW. That panel area exceeds the remaining roof area; a ground array, canopy or shared array would be required. This is why the project does not present full-time electric heating as a drop-in off-grid service.
