Cross-Wing House

A single-storey T-shaped home. The long living wing runs east-west; a perpendicular bedroom/studio wing abuts it to the south. The butterfly roof gives the home its original silhouette without an occupied upper floor. A shaded porch sits at the east living end.

Solar tilt and azimuth

39° tilt · 180° azimuth · year-round strategy. True south. Confirm magnetic declination, shading, snow shedding and structural fixing.

Roof: one 12° butterfly roof. Solar is mounted only on the south slope; the north slope has no array. Array azimuth is true south, independent of building rotation.

Ottawa reference: the year-round fixed range is 36–39° (SolarCalculator.CA reports 39° optimal), winter is about 60° and summer about 30°, facing true south within a few degrees. No energy production, snow-shedding, wind-uplift or code compliance is calculated.
