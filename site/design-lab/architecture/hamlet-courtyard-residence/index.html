Courtyard Residence

A long 40-foot module and a perpendicular 40-foot module enclose a private courtyard. Living, dining, kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and workspace open onto the courtyard and the surrounding woodland. The footprint is larger than the cottage, but the home remains single-storey and step-free. A flat green roof is shown as a concept only, with unresolved drainage and load questions.

Heat-pump assessment

Design heat load at −30 °C: 3.8 kW. Annual heating 5168 kWh thermal; annual cooling 1268 kWh. An air-source heat pump would use about 2721 kWh/year for heating and 575 kWh/year for cooling, in addition to the household load.

The existing household demand and reference array are unchanged. Full-time heat-pump heating would require about 55 additional winter-critical panels (109.8 m²), 100 kWh of additional battery for three days and a combined peak of 7.2 kW. That panel area exceeds the remaining roof area; a ground array, canopy or shared array would be required. This is why the project does not present full-time electric heating as a drop-in off-grid service.
