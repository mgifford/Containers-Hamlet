Solar Studio Stack

A compact 20-foot ground module holds essential living, a bathroom and a stair. A second 20-foot module sits above with a slight offset, providing a studio and sleeping space. The shed roof is solar-ready and the south facade is shaded by an overhang. The small footprint reduces ground coverage but adds stairs, structure and a more complex envelope.

Heat-pump assessment

Design heat load at −30 °C: 2.0 kW. Annual heating 2355 kWh thermal; annual cooling 743 kWh. An air-source heat pump would use about 1709 kWh/year for heating and 411 kWh/year for cooling, in addition to the household load.

The existing household demand and reference array are unchanged. Full-time heat-pump heating would require about 30 additional winter-critical panels (59.9 m²), 61 kWh of additional battery for three days and a combined peak of 4.4 kW. That panel area exceeds the remaining roof area; a ground array, canopy or shared array would be required. This is why the project does not present full-time electric heating as a drop-in off-grid service.
