Woodland Cottage

A single-storey 40-foot container cottage with an accessible internal route and washroom. The plan is deliberately simple: bedroom, bathroom, entrance hall, kitchen and living. The 2.8 m bathroom contains a modeled 1.5 m turning circle; the shared-path grades and professional accessibility review remain outstanding.

Solar tilt and azimuth

39° tilt · 180° azimuth · year-round strategy. True south. Confirm magnetic declination, shading, snow shedding and structural fixing.

Roof: one 24° gable roof with a clear ridge. Solar is mounted only on the south slope; the north slope has no array. Array azimuth is true south, independent of building rotation.

The array layout, tilt and azimuth shown in the drawings come from the same canonical energy model that calculates monthly generation. The tilt range 30–45° is an annual-production exploration range; 55–70° is the winter-oriented range. Orientation is measured from geographic north, independent of the SVG canvas or building rotation. Snow-shedding, wind uplift, roof structure and electrical design still require professional review.

Heat-pump assessment

Design heat load at −30 °C: 1.9 kW. Annual heating 2190 kWh thermal; annual cooling 690 kWh. An air-source heat pump would use about 1637 kWh/year for heating and 394 kWh/year for cooling, in addition to the household load.

The existing household demand and reference array are unchanged. Full-time heat-pump heating would require about 33 additional winter-critical panels (65.9 m²), 58 kWh of additional battery for three days and a combined peak of 3.9 kW. That panel area exceeds the remaining roof area; a ground array, canopy or shared array would be required. This is why the project does not present full-time electric heating as a drop-in off-grid service.
