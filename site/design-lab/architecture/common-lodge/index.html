Common Lodge

Three reused 40-foot containers in deep green, with yellow-framed glazing and a tall glazed central hall, frame a community lodge beside the water. One gable roof with a clear ridge covers the whole building; the solar array is mounted only on the south slope and is aligned to true south independently of the building. The north wing holds the shared kitchen, pantry, laundry and accessible washrooms; the central hall is the shared dining and gathering space; the south wing holds the workshop, meeting room and boot room; the west wing holds the café servery, office and a universal washroom. A screened veranda and covered entrance connect the hall to the forest. The existing authoritative lodge model is preserved separately; this is an additional Design Lab concept.

Foundation and ground

helical-piles · 0.6 m clearance · no compaction intent · independent decks: yes.

Elevated floor on engineered helical piles. Confirm soil, bedrock, frost uplift, corrosion, lateral capacity, slope stability and permit requirements before construction. Geotechnical, frost-uplift, corrosion, lateral, slope, permit and conservation-authority review are required. No fill, slab or compaction is the intent; the construction sequence still needs an arborist and erosion-control plan.

Solar tilt and azimuth

39° tilt · 180° azimuth · year-round strategy. Array is aligned to true south regardless of building rotation; it sits only on the south roof slope. Snow shedding, wind uplift, roof structure and electrical design need review.

Roof: one 10° gable roof with a clear ridge. Solar is mounted only on the south slope; the north slope has no array. Array azimuth is true south, independent of building rotation.

Ottawa reference: the year-round fixed range is 36–39° (SolarCalculator.CA reports 39° optimal), winter is about 60° and summer about 30°, facing true south within a few degrees. No energy production, snow-shedding, wind-uplift or code compliance is calculated.
