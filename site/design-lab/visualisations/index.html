These images are useful for atmosphere and massing. They are not model-verified exterior or interior records. They are published so people can visualise the direction while the next set is corrected.

AI-assisted concept visualisations. They are not construction drawings, measured perspectives, accessibility evidence or proof of ventilation, structure or code compliance. Known missing items are listed below; do not use v1 as a specification.

Known gaps in v1

  • No exterior chimney even though the interior shows a wood stove. The stove also lacks a visible outside-air duct, hearth clearance and wall protection.
  • No ventilation terminals: no HRV/ERV intake/exhaust, bathroom exhaust, ducted kitchen hood or transfer grilles.
  • No service core: no meter/disconnect, panel, inverter/battery, water heater, heat-pump outdoor unit, plumbing vent or solar conduit.
  • No accessible bathroom view. The images cannot demonstrate the 1.5 m turning circle, door width, grab-bar blocking or curbless shower.
  • The container is hidden. The building reads as a conventional timber cabin; corner castings, module joints or an expressed steel frame are absent.
  • Geometry is not reconciled. Gable window size, porch depth, roof edge, foundation piles and opening positions need checking against the scene/model.
  • Fire, snow and water detailing are missing: no gutters/downspouts shown consistently, no snow guards, no ember-resistant vent screens or defensible-space strategy.

Version 2 completion checklist

  1. Reconcile geometry: use the canonical scene for modules, storeys, openings, roof, porch, foundation and camera. No invented windows, doors, lofts or furniture.
  2. Show the ventilation path: two 200 mm insulated sleeves at opposite ends, low intake around 0.40 m, high exhaust, HRV/ERV, accessible filter, condensate drain, transfer grilles and bathroom undercut.
  3. Resolve combustion: insulated exterior chimney, roof clearance, spark cap, dedicated 75 mm stove OAK, non-combustible hearth/wall shield, CO and smoke alarms, firewood clearance.
  4. Resolve kitchen and bath: ducted range hood with makeup air, induction or documented gas supply, bathroom exhaust, 1.5 m turning circle, grab bars and curbless shower.
  5. Show the service core: meter/disconnect or labelled off-grid service, panel, inverter/battery, water heater, heat-pump unit, plumbing vent, shutoffs and access panels.
  6. Show solar/PV detail: conduit, rapid shutdown, flashed roof penetrations, snow guards and array-to-roof clearance.
  7. Resolve water and fire: gutters, downspouts, rain-barrel overflow, drainage away from piles, ember-resistant vents and a non-combustible edge.
  8. Prove accessibility: 1:12 ramp or level entrance, landings, 0.9 m doors, 1.2 m route, threshold, turning circle and bathroom fixtures.
  9. Show container identity: at least one corner casting, module joint or exposed steel frame; explain the timber rainscreen.
  10. Match the views: same sun/time, materials, window positions and porch geometry in exterior and interior images; no mismatched gable/roof.

Prompt additions for v2

Add these as structured facts, not as a vague style note. The prompt must require the terminals to be visible in the named view.

Visible services:
- HRV/ERV: low intake 0.40 m, high exhaust, two insulated 200 mm sleeves, opposite ends, accessible filter, condensate drain.
- Kitchen: ducted range hood with grease filter and makeup air; induction cooktop unless propane supply is shown.
- Bathroom: ducted exhaust, 1.5 m turning circle, 0.9 m door, grab-bar blocking, curbless shower.
- Wood stove: insulated exterior chimney above roof, 75 mm dedicated outside-air duct, non-combustible hearth/wall shield, CO/smoke alarms.
- Electrical: meter/disconnect or off-grid service, panel, inverter/battery, labelled access panels.
- Solar: conduit, rapid shutdown, flashed penetrations, snow guards.
- Water: gutters/downspouts, rain-barrel overflow, plumbing vent, shutoffs and cleanouts.
- Accessibility: 1:12 ramp or level entry, landings, 1.2 m route, threshold.
Negative prompt:
No unvented stove, no stove without chimney/OAK/hearth, no recirculating hood where ducted is required, no gas cooktop without propane/CO, no bathroom without exhaust/turning space, no hidden service core, no blank service wall, no missing guardrail/ramp, no solar without conduit/shutdown, no invented windows/doors/lofts.
