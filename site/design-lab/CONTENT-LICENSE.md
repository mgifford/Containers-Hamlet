## Third-party works

Do not assume external assets inherit the project's licences. Each imported
work must retain its original licence, copyright and attribution notices.
Record its source URL, author, version, modification history, and applicable
licence in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md. Verify compatibility before bundling.
If a work is licensed differently, identify its scope clearly in the UI and
in downloadable packages. Do not relicense third-party works without permission.

Architectural drawings are conceptual and have not been professionally verified.
