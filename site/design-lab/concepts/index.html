Common Lodge

A green container café-lodge under one south-facing shed roof.

Three reused 40-foot containers in deep green, with yellow-framed glazing and a tall glazed central hall, frame a community lodge beside the water. One gable roof with a clear ridge covers the whole building; the solar array is mounted only on the south slope and is aligned to true south independently of the building. The north wing holds the shared kitchen, pantry, laundry and accessible washrooms; the central hall is the shared dining and gathering space; the south wing holds the workshop, meeting room and boot room; the west wing holds the café servery, office and a universal washroom. A screened veranda and covered entrance connect the hall to the forest. The existing authoritative lodge model is preserved separately; this is an additional Design Lab concept.
