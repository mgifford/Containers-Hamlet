Containers Hamlet / Participate

An invitation, not a sales funnel

Help test the idea.

Containers Hamlet is open-source and exploratory. You can contribute a question, a critique, a drawing, a test or a source. You do not need to apply, disclose your income or circumstances, or join anything.

No contact database. There is no membership application, mailing list, account or tracking form. Do not post personal circumstances publicly.

Ways to contribute

Architectural concepts

Test a plan, section, porch or container arrangement against structure, fire, accessibility and buildability.

Accessibility

Review the homes, paths and shared facilities; identify barriers and propose redesigns.

Community governance

Compare ownership, membership, decision-making and dispute-resolution models.

Environmental stewardship

Review water, wastewater, fire, wetland, tree protection and material choices.

Financial models

Build transparent cost categories and challenge assumptions; do not submit personal financial data.

Planning research

Investigate a real jurisdiction, official plan, zoning and approval path.

Open-source development

Improve the static site, tests, JSON models and accessibility.

Usability testing

Run the testing plan with representative participants and report what failed.

How to contribute without being a developer

  1. Read the community introduction and choose one question.
  2. Open a GitHub issue with the question, evidence and what you would change.
  3. For a design or document change, describe the tradeoff and the source. Do not upload third-party images you do not have rights to use.
  4. For accessibility or usability, use the testing plan and report tasks, barriers and assistive technology used.

Privacy and boundaries

Do not include your address, health information, financial information, immigration status or other personal circumstances in a public issue. If a contribution needs private context, describe the issue without the personal detail. No contribution is a promise of housing, membership or investment.
