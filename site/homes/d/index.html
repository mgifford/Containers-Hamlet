Orientation #

The porch faces south-south-west over the beaver meadow, while the entry and path stay on the north side, away from the wetland.

Main view #

The beaver meadow: open sedge, alder and standing water, with the south knoll and its rock shoulder beyond. Bearing ~209°, ~51 m to the beaver meadow. partly wooded, with openings that reveal the water or sky.

Through alder, cedar and low meadow vegetation.

Nearby homes #

Cabin E is about 45 m north-east and screened by cedar; the lodge is about 45 m north and partly visible through trees in winter. In the view cone: No other building inside the principal view cone..

Path and entry #

The south path approaches from the north and ends at the north entry. It stays outside the 30 m wetland buffer, so nobody walks between the porch and the meadow.

Winter #

The meadow opens completely when the alder drops its leaves, and the low sun reaches the porch. Cedar on the east side screens the lodge.

Design note #

A seasonal home might use a smaller, simpler service core and a larger screened room. The wetland view is the reason for this site; the building should stay modest.
