Orientation #

The porch faces west-south-west to the lake across the southern cove. The entry is on the east and the path arrives from the north-east.

Main view #

The southern cove of Heron Lake through juniper and cedar, with the opposite shore close enough to read the rock. Bearing ~271°, ~50 m to Heron Lake. partly wooded, with openings that reveal the water or sky.

Through juniper, cedar and red pine.

Nearby homes #

Cabin C is about 60 m north and hidden by the shore knoll; no other home is visible from the porch. In the view cone: No other building inside the principal view cone..

Path and entry #

The Cabin F path arrives from the north-east at the east entry. It crosses a seasonal drainage channel on a proposed culvert and stays out of the shoreline buffer.

Winter #

Cedar and juniper keep the cove private. The lake opens wider in winter, and the low sun reaches the porch through the bare birch.
