The homes

Six cabins, one lodge, seven shareable pages

The map is a shared overview. Each home has its own page for the orientation, main view, path, winter notes and siting checks, with anchors so a particular section can be shared directly.

Fictional concepts. Placements, views and checks are illustrative. No land rights, building permission or privacy guarantee is implied.

A cedar-screened bench above the lake, with the porch turned to the water and the entry tucked behind.

Main view
Heron Lake seen through cedar and pine, with the far shore low on the horizon.
Orientation
