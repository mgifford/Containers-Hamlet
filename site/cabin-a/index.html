Cabin A · The Retreat

One 40-foot high-cube container, continuous external insulation, a screened porch and a graded trail approach. Concept only; not construction drawings.

Not engineered. Dimensions, openings, structure, fire separation, accessibility and energy performance require professional review. See the Look inside explorer for the room model.

Facts and intent

Overall size
~12.6 × 2.8 m outside the rainscreen (~36 m² footprint)
Modules
1 × 40-foot high-cube ISO container, 12.192 × 2.438 × 2.896 m
Clear floor area
~27 m² inside the lining, plus ~11 m² of porch and deck
Clear height
2.65 m in the modules
Floor above grade
~0.6 m on piles, with a graded gravel trail/landing at 1:15; the longest section is 9.6 m, not a timber walkway
Exterior wall
280 mm: lining, steel, 150 mm external insulation, ventilated cavity, timber rainscreen
Roof PV (concept)
10 modules, about 4.2 kWp, drawn as individual tilted panels; sizing needs measured solar resource and winter loads
