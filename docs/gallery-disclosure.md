# Gallery arrangements and page disclosure

## Arrangement studies

The Design Lab gallery now includes four arrangement studies alongside the ten concept cards:

- **Stacked pair** — vertical home, external stair, fire separation and second exit.
- **Cantilever** — upper module over a sheltered entry; back-span, connections, uplift and snow drift.
- **L / courtyard** — two wings around a shared deck.
- **Creek-spanning room** — clear span outside the banks; flood/scour, debris and conservation review.

These are explicitly **possible, not offered as engineered models**. Each card links to its structural, egress or site notes. They use original diagrams and are not presented as editable or buildable.

## Less page density

- Concept specification lists are collapsed behind **Dimensions, access and performance**.
- Gallery view/source panels default closed.
- Fire building/code/model sections collapse.
- Resilience climate and indicator tables collapse.
- Water greywater, blackwater, fire-reserve, drought and source sections collapse.
- Two-storey structure, fire, envelope and model sections collapse.

Primary warnings and decisions stay visible. `<details>` works without JavaScript and keeps the content available to search engines and assistive technology.
