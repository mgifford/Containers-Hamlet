# Pattern lens annotations

## Status

Phase 4 adds an initial **pattern lens** to the Design Lab architecture pages for:

- **Woodland Cottage** — five annotations using authored room polygons;
- **Accessible Garden Home** — five annotations using authored room polygons;
- **Common lodge** — text-only annotations, because it has no authored interior room model.

The lens covers:

- 127 · Intimacy Gradient;
- 112 · Entrance Transition;
- 159 · Light on Two Sides of Every Room;
- 163 · Outdoor Room;
- 180 · Window Place.

## What the lens does

- Select a pattern to highlight the relevant room polygon in the authored interior plan.
- Lists observations, limits, status and an evaluation question.
- Links back to the full pattern entry.
- Provides all annotations in a details list without JavaScript.
- Uses stable room IDs (`#room-w-hall`, `#room-e-living`, etc.).
- Keeps the plan and text available without JavaScript.
- States that the lens is a reading aid, not a finding.

## What it does not do

- Prove that a pattern is satisfied.
- Measure daylight, privacy, acoustics, views or resident satisfaction.
- Model door swings, furniture, sightlines or daylight factors.
- Show the porch/deck on the interior plan; those are noted as text-only or shown on the building views.
- Fabricate a lodge interior. The lodge annotations are explicitly text-only.
- Cover all six homes yet. The other four homes need room-level annotations and plan IDs before a lens can be added.
- Replace professional accessibility, fire, structural or architectural review.

## Status vocabulary

- **conceptual** — a design reading, not a measured result.
- **evidence-needed** — the plan lacks the information needed to evaluate it.
- **not-evaluated** — no daylight, privacy, acoustic or resident study exists.
