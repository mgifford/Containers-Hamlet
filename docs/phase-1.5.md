# Phase 1.5: credible community

This phase shifts the site from an architectural showcase toward a resident-facing exploration. The architecture and models remain central; the new material explains what living in the community would require.

## Added

- `/community/` — what the hamlet is, what is private, what is shared and what is unresolved.
- `/community/scenarios/` — six illustrative resident situations linked to existing homes.
- `/community/ownership/` — land, home, lease, membership, association and infrastructure layers; decision-making and protections.
- `/community/costs/` — cost categories and a user-entered worksheet. No invented prices.
- `/community/roadmap/` — ten-stage feasibility sequence with questions, responsible parties, evidence, blockers and dependencies.
- `/living/` — winter, access, water, wastewater, guests, pets, wildlife, privacy, conflict and accessibility.
- `/participate/` — contribution paths without a membership form, mailing list or personal disclosure.
- `/community/usability/` — a proposed testing plan and results template; no completed-test claim.
- `/licensing/` and `LICENSING.md` — current/intended licence matrix and no automatic relicensing.
- Homepage resident-introduction section and top-level navigation.

## Deliberately not done

- No legal agreement, lease, membership, price, construction cost, financing commitment or planning approval.
- No contact database, application, mailing list or tracking.
- No claim that usability testing has been completed.
- No relicensing of existing MIT/CC BY material.
- No invented construction or land prices.
- No vertical stacked home added to Look inside.

## Accessibility follow-up: Cedar Accessible

`/homes/Cedar-Accessible/` is a two-option layout study, not a seventh home or an accessibility certification.

- **Option A, one 40-foot module:** one-person layout; full-width bathroom; 1.5 m turning in kitchen/living; single bed with a 1.5 m transfer side. A double bed with transfer space does not fit across 2.35 m clear width.
- **Option B, two 40-foot modules:** one-bedroom layout with a double bed and transfer space; larger bathroom and circulation. Requires a structural opening/transfer frame and fire separation.
- Both: 0.9 m door targets, step-free porch, reachable storage, a firm winter route and a place to wait for help.
- Still unresolved: structural opening, fire/egress, foundation/drainage, fixture clearances, personal care, CSA B651/code review and consultant sign-off.
- Linked from the Cedar home page and `/living/#accessibility`.
