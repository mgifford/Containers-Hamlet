# Review: Solar Studio Stack candidates and the six-design board

**Status:** Review complete. **None of the six Solar Studio Stack collages is
publishable.** Candidate **B** is the closest base because it shows the more usable of
the two visible stairs, even though it is the wrong stair type. The model's massing,
offset, solar array, cove setting, upper metal/lower timber and no-balcony
condition are right; the failures are the **gable roof instead of the 12° shed**,
the **stair** (missing in four, conventional in two, never the recorded
alternating-tread), the **furnished interiors** (the interior is a provisional
zone study, and the upper ceiling is drawn sloping), the **added lakeside deck**,
the **missing bathroom**, the thin reveals and slab base, and the absent
captions.

**Subject:** six 1536 × 1024 collages, each a 3 × 2 set: exterior, living,
kitchen, workspace, bedroom, deck. Labels map to the file suffixes:

## The model in one paragraph

Solar Studio Stack is **two 20 ft modules**: `m1` timber at 0.6 m and `m2` metal
at 3.896 m, offset **0.8 m east**. The roof is a **solar-ready shed at 12°** with
a 0.9 m overhang and **installed PV**. The covered terrace `p1` is
**4.4 × 2.4 m** on the south face, shaded by the upper offset and overhang. The
ground floor has a 2.0 m living slider, a 0.95 m entry, a 1.2 m kitchen window
and a 0.8 m bathroom window. The upper floor has 2.4 × 1.6 m studio glazing, a
1.2 m north window and a 0.9 × 2.1 m **east door with no balcony**. The stair is
a **compact alternating-tread**, 0.9 m wide, 2.6 m run, 2.9 m rise, **no
landing**. The interior is a **provisional zone study** — one open 6.06 × 2.44 m
volume, no partitions, no fixtures, a **flat underside**, no sloping ceiling.
The site is **juniper and cedar with the opposite shore of the cove close enough
to read the rock**. There is **no deck**; the terrace is the outdoor space. Snow
guards are unresolved. The envelope adds **205 mm**; the modules sit at 0.6 m.

## What the set gets right

- **Two 20 ft modules, stacked with an offset.** The upper metal box sits over
  the lower timber box with the 0.8 m shift. The signature massing is present.
- **The solar array.** Every candidate shows PV on the roof. This is the first
  set where the energy story is consistently visible.
- **The cove.** The lake, the rocky opposite shore, the juniper and cedar are
  right for the site. This is the best site match of any set so far.
- **The upper metal/lower timber pair.** The materials read correctly.
- **The covered terrace** with chairs and a table.
- **No upper balcony.** Every candidate keeps the east door balcony-free.
- **No snow guards.** Correct, because they are unresolved.
- **No stove, chimney, third storey or wide cabin.**
- **A coherent set** with consistent light, materials and lake. No people, text
  or watermark.

## Shared failures

### H — the roof is a gable, not a 12° shed

Every candidate shows a two-plane gable with a ridge and PV on one slope. The
model is a **single-plane shed at 12°** with a 0.9 m overhang. The roof form is
the home's most distinctive element after the stack, and the set replaces it.

### H — the stair is missing or the wrong type

Only B and C show a stair. B's is a conventional straight stair with a landing;
C's is an open-riser stair with a rail. A, D, E and F do not show a stair at all.
The model's stair is a **compact alternating-tread, 0.9 × 2.6 m, no landing**,
and the model carries an explicit warning that alternating-tread stairs are
harder to use and need code, handrail and egress review.

The two candidates that show a stair silently replace the warned type with a
conventional one. The four that omit it remove the only access to the upper
studio and sleeping zone. Neither is acceptable.

### H — the interiors are furnished room layouts

The interior is one open 6.06 × 2.44 m volume with no partitions, no fixtures, a
flat underside and no sloping ceiling. The interior hero's `mustNotShow` forbids
partitions, doors, a defined sleeping alcove, a bed, desk, sofa or other
fixture, a balcony/guardrail, a sloping or butterfly ceiling, a kitchen hood,
service core or HRV terminal, and a room-layout claim.

Every collage shows a furnished living room, a fitted kitchen with a fridge and
cabinets, a workspace with a desk **and a single bed**, a bedroom with a double
bed, and a sloped wood ceiling throughout the upper floor. The sloped ceiling is
itself an explicit exclusion. The workspace bed is a second sleeping place that
the model does not contain.

### H — the lakeside deck is not modelled

The model has **no deck**; the covered terrace `p1` is 4.4 × 2.4 m. Every
candidate shows a large lakeside deck with chairs. The deck is a new outdoor
platform and a new structural/guarding condition.

### M — the bathroom is not shown

The model has a ground-floor bathroom with a 0.8 m window. No candidate shows a
bathroom, in a set that otherwise presents the whole home.

### H — the finished envelope is missing

- The windows and doors sit nearly flush with the cladding; no ~205 mm reveal is
  legible.
- The modules sit on a slab or at grade; the 0.6 m undercroft and piles are
  absent.
- The roof edge is thin; the 250 mm roof and 200 mm floor insulation are not
  visible.
- The result reads as a thin-skinned box on a slab.

### M — no gutter, downspout, ember vent or defensible space

The exterior requires a gutter and one downspout on the low south eave, closed
ember-resistant vents and defensible planting. None is visible, and the shrubs
and rocks press against the walls and terrace.

### M — windows larger than the schedule

The living slider is about 3 m against the recorded 2.0 m; the kitchen window is
about 1.8 m against 1.2 m; the upper studio window is wide but close to the
recorded 2.4 × 1.6 m; the bedroom window is not in the upper opening list. The
set moves toward a glazed cabin rather than the compact container.

### M — snow guards and roof shedding

The model excludes installed snow guards and records the snow-shedding risk over
the entry and terrace as unresolved. The candidates show no guards, which is
correct, but the roof edge and the entry below it read as resolved. This needs a
caption, not a fix.

### H — no captions, scene hash or review status

None carries the required caption, scene hash `56dd76bb`, review status, known
deviations or the "illustrative interpretation, not a construction drawing"
label.

## The six-design board

The overview board is a good **index**: six numbered cards, a one-line
description for each home, and a disclaimer that says "Concept visualizations
only. Not verified construction drawings; geometry may differ from canonical
models." That disclaimer is necessary and correctly worded.

It is not yet a publishable selected-home board, because:

- **All six thumbnails are unverified candidate images, and several carry
  known hard failures.** The Woodland card has no PV; the Offset card puts the
  porch under the cantilever; the Courtyard card has black metal cladding and
  oversized glazing; the Cross-Stacked card has a red metal upper and no PV; the
  Accessible card is the U plan without the accessible bathroom; the Solar card
  is a gable, not the shed.
- **The Solar card contradicts its own description.** The card says "Two compact
  levels, solar shed roof"; the thumbnail is a two-plane gable.
- **The Accessible card says "Single-storey garden-oriented design"**, which is
  true, but the image is a U plan that omits the 3.1 m bathroom and the 1.2 m
  route.
- **The Cross-Stacked card says "Perpendicular stacked containers"**, which is
  true of the massing, but the image has the wrong upper material and no PV.
- **The board does not carry the specific known deviations** for any thumbnail.
  "Geometry may differ" is a blanket caveat; the project's own review standard
  asks for the specific deviations.

Recommendation: keep the board format and the disclaimer, but either (a) use the
canonical drawings for the six cards, or (b) add a short known-deviation line to
each card and mark the set "concept only — not the selected-home visualisations".
Fix the Solar card's "shed roof" description or the image.

**The three referenced PDFs** at
`~/Downloads/containers_hamlet_six_designs*.pdf` are not present in this
environment; the Downloads folder is empty and no matching file exists in the
workspace or attachment store. They could not be reviewed. Re-attach them if
they contain something different from the board PNG.

## What would make a publishable Solar set

1. **Use the site-facing reference** (`site-facing.svg`) and the exact modules:
   `m1` timber at 0.6 m, `m2` metal at 3.896 m, offset 0.8 m east.
2. **Show the 12° shed roof** with the 0.9 m overhang and PV, not a gable.
3. **Keep the terrace 4.4 × 2.4 m** and remove the lakeside deck.
4. **Resolve the stair:** show the alternating-tread 0.9 × 2.6 m with its
   warning in the caption, or change the model and record the new stair; do not
   silently swap in a conventional stair.
5. **Make the interior a zone study:** one open 6.06 × 2.44 m volume, no
   partitions, fixtures, beds, desks, sofa or kitchen; a flat underside, not a
   sloped ceiling; the upper east door with no balcony.
6. **Show the ground bathroom** with its 0.8 m window.
7. **Use the exact openings:** 2.0 m living slider, 0.95 m entry, 1.2 m kitchen
   window, 0.8 m bathroom window, 2.4 × 1.6 m studio glazing, 1.2 m north
   window, 0.9 × 2.1 m east door.
8. **Show the finished envelope:** 205 mm reveals, 0.6 m piles, 250 mm roof,
   200 mm floor, corner casting, gutter and downspout, ember vent, defensible
   planting.
9. **Keep the cove and juniper/cedar site**, with the opposite shore close
   enough to read the rock.
10. **Caption every image** with scene hash `56dd76bb`, review status, known
    deviations and "not a construction drawing"; note that snow guards are
    unresolved.

## Recommendation

- **Reject all six as selected-home visualisations.** The massing, solar array,
  cove and no-balcony condition are right; the roof, stair, interiors, deck and
  missing bathroom are not.
- **Use B as the base** because it has the more usable of the two visible
  stairs, then change the roof to the 12° shed, resolve the stair honestly, remove the
  deck, restore the zone-study interior, and add the bathroom and envelope
  details.
- **Use C only after the open-riser stair is replaced.** Use A, D, E and F only
  after a stair is added; none can stand without upper-floor access.
- **Keep the six-design board as an index, not a published set.** Use the
  canonical drawings or add the specific known deviations to each card; fix the
  Solar "shed" description or the thumbnail.
- **Re-attach the three PDFs** if they contain a different board or drawing set;
  they were not available here.

## Limits of this review

- Visual comparison only; no photogrammetry, lens calibration or camera match.
- No provider, prompt or seed metadata was supplied.
- No structural, energy, fire, plumbing or code assessment is made or implied.
- The alternating-tread stair is a known usability and code concern in the
  model; its absence or replacement in the images is not a design approval.
- The set is treated as six candidates for one home. If any candidate belongs to
  another design, the roof, stair, interior and site gates still apply, but the
  home-specific verdict would not.
- The three referenced PDFs were not present at the given paths and could not be
  read.
