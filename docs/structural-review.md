# Structural review

## What this document is

A **structured review register** for every Design Lab concept. It records the
questions that still need professional work. It is not an engineering
assessment, a code opinion, a fire-resistance rating or a construction method
statement.

The register is generated by `Model.structuralReview()` in
`site/js/design-model.js` and shown in the editor, the architectural pages and
the hamlet-wide comparison.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning | Who can assign it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `not-assessed` | The concern does not currently apply, or no assessment exists. | The model, by default. |
| `requires-review` | A trigger applies. A qualified professional must review it. | The model, when a geometric or data trigger is present. |
| `conceptually-resolved` | A design intention or note resolves the concept-level question. | A designer, with a note. Still not verification. |
| `professionally-verified` | A named professional has reviewed the concern. | Only with reviewer, organisation, date, scope and document reference. |

Automatic generation never assigns `professionally-verified`. The schema rejects
a verified claim without a documented, attributable assessment, and the geometry
validator adds a review warning if the review register claims verification
without evidence.

A `requires-review` row is **not a defect**. It is an honest statement that the
concept has not been engineered. A blank or `not-assessed` row is **not an
approval**.

## Concerns and triggers

| Concern | Trigger in the model | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Corner castings and corner posts | Always | Lifting, openings and stacked loads concentrate force at the corner castings and posts. |
| Vertical load transfer | Any upper module | A continuous load path through posts, beams or a transfer structure is required. |
| Container orientation and span | Always | Container side walls are not designed as beams. Orientation and openings change behaviour. |
| Openings through sidewalls | Openings ≥ 2.0 m or sliding doors, or any wall removal | Large openings need engineered framing, headers and corner reinforcement. |
| Removed structural walls | `structure.wall_removal` other than `none` | A removed sidewall changes the load path and may need a transfer frame. |
| Cantilevers and unsupported overhangs | Upper module with support fraction < 1 | The model reports unsupported area; no arbitrary cantilever limit is treated as acceptable. |
| Lateral stability and racking | Stacking or multiple modules | Stacked or linked modules need a designed lateral-force system. |
| Wind loading and uplift | Stacking or height > 4 m | Exposed sites, overhangs, cladding and stacked modules need uplift and lateral review. |
| Snow loading and drift | Always | Roof geometry, valleys, parapets and adjacent volumes change accumulation and drift. |
| Foundation reactions | Stacking, multiple modules or a green roof | Point loads, uplift and frost-depth requirements change. |
| Connections between modules | More than one module | Inter-module connections need shear, tension and bearing capacity. |
| Crane access and lifting | Multiple modules or a 40-foot module | Lifting points, reach, ground bearing, matting and overhead clearance need a lift plan. |
| Transport limitations | 40-foot module or insulated width > 3.0 m | Oversize width, route surveys, permits and escorts may be required. |
| Fire separation and egress | Stacking or multiple modules | Inter-storey separation, protected egress, cladding, cavity barriers and fire access need review. |
| Corrosion protection | Always | Ground contact, de-icing, concealed steel and coastal exposure need a strategy. |
| Thermal bridges | Always | Steel posts, floor edges, connections and penetrations create thermal bridges and condensation risk. |
| Moisture and condensation | Always | Interstitial condensation, trapped water, roof drainage and floor build-ups need an envelope review. |

## What the model calculates

The model calculates:

* plan polygons and overlap areas
* module base and top elevations
* vertical gaps
* support-overlap fraction
* building footprint and gross floor area
* roof top elevation and roof area
* whether a trigger is present

It does **not** calculate:

* load capacity, member sizes or connection design
* lateral-force distribution or diaphragm action
* wind or snow loads
* foundation reactions, frost uplift or geotechnical capacity
* crane loads, outrigger pressure or lift planning
* fire-resistance ratings or egress time
* corrosion rates or moisture accumulation

## Interpreting the six homes

| Home | Structural character | Main review issues |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Woodland Cottage | Single-storey gable | Openings, roof-to-wall connection, foundation, wind uplift. |
| Offset Pair | Two ground modules, 2.6 m stagger | Independent foundations, shared link, roof junction, wind uplift. |
| Courtyard Residence | Two ground modules, L-shape, partial wall removal | Corner connection, large opening framing, flat green roof loads and drainage. |
| Cross-Wing House | Two ground modules, T-wing | Junction transfer, roof valley, lateral stability, foundation on slope. |
| Accessible Garden Home | Two ground 20-foot modules | Shared-wall connection, wide opening framing, canopy uplift. |
| Solar Studio | Two ground 20-foot modules, 0.8 m offset | Shared connection, roof PV fixing, uplift, compact foundation. |

## Professional evidence

A verified concern must carry an assessment like:

```json
{
  "status": "professionally-verified",
  "assessment": {
    "reviewer": "Name",
    "organisation": "Engineering practice",
    "date": "2026-11-20",
    "scope": "Bearing, connections and lateral stability for the concept only",
    "document": "report-or-drawing-reference"
  }
}
```

The project does not invent these records. A concept exported from the editor
contains `verified: false` and `status: requires-review` by default.

## Limits of the review page

The architectural pages show the status and the reason for each concern. They do
not show calculations because none exist. Any future engineering work must be
added with its scope, assumptions, author and document reference, and it must not
be presented as a permit or a certification unless that is what it is.
