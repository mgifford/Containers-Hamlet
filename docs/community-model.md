# Proposed community model (not legal advice)

A community land trust holds the parcel and grants long-term, transferable ground leases where law permits. Residents may hold homes separately, but treatment of buildings and improvements varies by jurisdiction. A resident association manages day-to-day shared systems. Proposed initial mix: three permanent homes, two seasonal homes, one community rental. Other residents could seek permission for short-term letting, with local permits, insurance, guest limits, noise and fire rules, and a contribution to common reserves. A permanent-resident majority and anti-speculation protections should be debated before adoption. Housing security, accessibility accommodations, due process, transparent fees, and ecological safeguards should be explicit. Do not publish a real lease until local legal review.
