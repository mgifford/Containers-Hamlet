# Proposed community model (not legal advice, not a lease)

A community land trust would hold the fictional parcel and grant long-term, transferable ground leases where law permits. Residents may own their dwellings separately, but the treatment of buildings and improvements varies by jurisdiction. A residents' association would manage day-to-day shared systems. The proposed initial mix is three permanent homes, two seasonal homes and one community-operated rental. Other residents could seek permission for short-term letting, subject to local permits, insurance, guest limits, noise and fire rules, and a contribution to common reserves.

A permanent-resident majority and anti-speculation protections should be debated before adoption. Housing security, accessibility accommodations, due process, transparent fees and ecological safeguards should be explicit. The trust's legal form, membership, resale formula, dispute resolution, rental policy, water and wastewater servicing, energy tariffs, road maintenance, snow clearing, fire response and insurance are all unresolved.

This page describes a direction for discussion. It does not create a lease, a price, a tenancy, a membership or a legal relationship, and it must not be published as a real offer without local legal review.
