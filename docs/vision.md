# A lake, a forest, a small community

This is a speculative Canadian lakeside hamlet of six modular homes and a common lodge on a fictional 15-acre site. Three homes are imagined as permanent residences, two as seasonal homes, and one as a community-operated rental. Residents might own their homes and lease sites from a community land trust, subject to provincial legal feasibility. The trust would preserve land and common infrastructure; a residents' association could operate shared facilities. Participation is an invitation to explore, not a solicitation to rent or invest.

Each home has a bug-screened outdoor room. Vehicles approach a shared parking/service area; paths provide the final approach, with accessible drop-off and emergency access still to be designed. The wetland is protected, not drained. The shoreline remains vegetated. The forest is a design constraint, not a vacant canvas.
